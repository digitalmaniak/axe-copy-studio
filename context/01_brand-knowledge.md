## The Guiding Question

Before writing any copy, ask:

**"How does this technology make life feel better, easier, more immersive, or more meaningful?"**

Not "What does this technology do?" — that is a feature list. Every piece of LG copy answers
the first question, not the second.

---

## Brand Positioning

**Brand:** LG Electronics USA · **Tagline:** Life's Good

LG is a premium lifestyle technology brand built around better living through thoughtful
innovation. It occupies the space between luxury and accessibility: elevated but never
exclusionary, intelligent but never intimidating. LG positions technology as ease, immersion,
connection, and possibility — not as complexity.

---

## Core Brand Pillars

1. **Innovation With Purpose** — innovation must create meaningful improvement, not just
   technical advancement. Focus on outcomes, transformation, ease, emotional payoff. Avoid
   feature dumping and engineering-first language.
2. **Elevated Everyday Experiences** — LG turns ordinary routines into premium experiences.
   *The experience enabled by the product is the hero*, not the product alone. Center copy on
   atmosphere, feeling, immersion, comfort, flow.
3. **Premium Without Pretension** — polished, confident, contemporary, aspirational, welcoming.
   Never snobbish, sterile, or exclusive for its own sake. **LG is premium for real life.**
4. **Human-Centered Intelligence** — AI feels intuitive and natural — invisible assistance, not
   futuristic spectacle. Preferred framing: "adapts to you," "takes out the guesswork,"
   "personalized," "optimized automatically." Avoid robotic / sci-fi / algorithm-heavy language.
5. **Trusted Performance & Reliability** — established, proven, trustworthy, without sounding
   conservative or boastful. Use assurance, consistency, proven leadership. Avoid aggressive
   superiority claims.

## Tone of Voice

**Four words: Confident. Immersive. Human. Refined.** The brand speaks with confidence, not noise.

- Premium but approachable · Emotionally resonant but concise · Polished but natural · Aspirational but grounded

**Tone characteristics:**

1. **Benefit-First, Feature-Second** — features support the story, they are not the story.
   - Weak: "Features AI processing and advanced brightness technology."
   - Stronger: "Brilliant brightness and intelligent picture optimization bring every scene to life."
2. **Immersive and Sensory** — language tied to cinema, energy, clarity, immersion, realism,
   atmosphere. Especially TV, audio, gaming.
3. **Clean and Modern** — streamlined, contemporary sentences. No clutter, qualifiers, corporate
   filler, or jargon.
4. **Aspirational Realism** — elevated but attainable. The reader should think "that could
   genuinely improve my everyday life," not "that's fantasy luxury."

## Messaging Hierarchy

Structure any copy block in this order:

1. **Emotional Outcome** — what does this *feel* like? (cinematic immersion / effortless cooking)
2. **Real-World Benefit** — how does life improve? (faster cleaning / brighter daylight viewing)
3. **Supporting Technology** — what enables it? (AI processors / OLED / TurboWash / Dolby Atmos)

## Preferred Vocabulary

- **Tone words:** immersive · vivid · brilliant · seamless · elevated · cinematic · effortlessly ·
  personalized · ultra-smooth · beautifully designed · intelligently optimized
- **Power verbs:** delivers · enhances · intelligently adapts · transforms · unlocks · elevates ·
  completes · flows · expands
- **Opener verb "Enjoy":** "Enjoy exceptional vocal clarity" / "Enjoy effortless cooking"
- **Numbers for credibility:** always pair a number with a benefit ("12 speaker drivers and 8
  passive radiators," "97 inches of pure OLED").

## Writing Guardrails

### Words & patterns to avoid

| Avoid | Why / instead |
|-------|---------------|
| amazing, incredible | Empty hype → breathtaking, exceptional, vivid |
| game-changing | Overused → describe the actual change |
| innovative / innovation (standalone) | Vague → name the specific technology |
| cutting-edge, state-of-the-art | Generic → describe the advancement |
| best in class | Unproven → prove with a specific differentiator |
| redefine your lifestyle | Empty luxury → be specific about what changes |
| next-level, ultimate experience | Meaningless without specificity |
| solutions, leveraging, ecosystem synergies | Corporate jargon — never |
| Passive voice ("is delivered by") | Active voice only ("delivers") |
| Ellipsis / em-dashes in subject lines | Not permitted |
| Trademark symbols (™, ®) | Only when legally required |

### Brand voice anti-patterns
- **Cold Tech Brand** — too analytical/spec-heavy. Never a datasheet.
- **Luxury Fashion Brand** — too exclusive/abstract/editorial. LG is premium for real life, not runway.
- **Discount Retail Brand** — too loud/salesy. Price is not the lead unless it's a promo workflow.
- **Generic AI Copy** — "elevate / next level / redefine / seamless experience" with no specificity.

### Legal / compliance
- Offer claims ($/% off) must match the actual offer exactly — never approximate.
- "Free installation" integrated naturally in body copy, not a standalone badge.
- Availability/offer qualifiers compress into subtext — do not omit.
- No unverified superlatives ("world's best," "only") without confirmed sourcing.

## Gold-standard copy examples (real LG voice — grow this list)

> These are **approved, on-voice** LG headline/body pairs. They anchor the copy generator to LG's
> real cadence (rhythm-of-three, two-part contrast, benefit-first, concrete proof points). **The
> copywriting team should keep adding real approved lines here** — the biggest remaining gaps are
> **eyebrows** and **legal-disclaimer** examples (savings/%-off headlines now have a few). Match the
> *spirit* (voice, rhythm, specificity), never copy verbatim. Use **"XX%"** as the placeholder when
> the exact discount isn't known yet (it's swapped for the real number at layout time).

*What these teach: rhythm-of-three ("Touch it. Talk to it. Take it anywhere."), two-part contrast ("Not just bigger. Infinitely better."), benefit-first bodies opening on experience, and hard proof points woven in (94/100, 4.5 hrs, 400+ channels, up to 100" 4K, $ values). Keep numbers concrete and the offer/legal compressed inline.*
